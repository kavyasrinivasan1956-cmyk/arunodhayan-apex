Why I build

From engineering detail to meaningful impact.

My career began in mechanical engineering, where precision, reliability and disciplined problem-solving shaped how I approached product development. As my responsibilities grew, I became increasingly interested in the larger questions behind every product: which customer problem are we solving, how does the portfolio create value, and what choices will keep it relevant tomorrow?

That curiosity led me from core development into product management, global portfolio leadership, project management and product line management. Today, I bring those perspectives together in the automotive connectivity space.

Electric and software-defined vehicles are changing the architecture of mobility. I am especially interested in how product strategy, electronics and dependable connections can help make that transition safer, smarter and more sustainable.
